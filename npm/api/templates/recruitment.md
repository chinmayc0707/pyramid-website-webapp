## Our Recruitment Process

### 1. Discovery & Role Definition
Deep dive into your requirements, culture, and success criteria. We go beyond job descriptions to understand what truly drives performance.

### 2. Sourcing & Attraction
Multi-channel sourcing strategy: our proprietary database, professional networks, headhunting, job boards, social media, and referral programs.

### 3. Screening & Assessment
Rigorous multi-stage evaluation: competency interviews, technical assessments, psychometric testing, cultural fit evaluation, and reference verification.

### 4. Shortlist & Presentation
Curated shortlist of 3-5 qualified candidates with detailed assessment reports, salary expectations, and availability.

### 5. Interview Coordination
Seamless scheduling, interviewer briefing packs, feedback collection, and candidate communication management.

### 6. Offer & Onboarding
Offer negotiation, background checks, contract generation, and pre-start engagement to ensure day-one readiness.
