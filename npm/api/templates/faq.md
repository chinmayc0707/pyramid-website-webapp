## Outsourcing

### What's the difference between BPO and HRO?
**BPO (Business Process Outsourcing):** Non-HR business processes — finance, customer service, IT support, data processing, supply chain.

**HRO (HR Outsourcing):** HR-specific processes — payroll, benefits, compliance, employee relations, HRIS administration, recruitment support.

**Many clients use both** for a unified operations partnership.

### How does payroll outsourcing work?
1. **Setup:** We configure your payroll rules, earnings/deductions, tax registrations
2. **Each Cycle:** You provide hours/changes → We process → You approve → We distribute payslips & file taxes
3. **Compliance:** We handle all statutory filings, year-end forms, audit support
4. **Support:** Employee self-service portal, dedicated payroll specialist

**Frequency:** Weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly — multi-country, multi-currency.

### What's the transition timeline for outsourcing?
**Typical timeline:**
- **Assessment & Design:** 2-4 weeks
- **Knowledge Transfer:** 4-8 weeks
- **Parallel Run:** 2-4 payroll cycles / 1-2 months operations
- **Full Cutover:** Week 12-16

**We manage change carefully** — communication plans, stakeholder alignment, risk mitigation at each phase.

### Is my data secure?
**Yes.** We're ISO 27001 & SOC 2 Type II certified. Data protections include:
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) & in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Role-based access control & audit logging
- Annual penetration testing
- GDPR, CCPA, PDPA compliance
- Data processing agreements standard
- Right to audit clause in every contract

### Can I keep some processes in-house?
Absolutely. **Hybrid models** are common:
- You keep strategy & employee relations; we do payroll & compliance
- You keep recruiting; we do onboarding & HR administration
- You keep HRIS admin; we do benefits & leave management

We design the split based on your strengths and our value-add.
