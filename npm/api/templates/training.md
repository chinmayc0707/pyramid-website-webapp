## Learning Outcomes & Measurement

We use the **Kirkpatrick Model** to measure training effectiveness:

- **Level 1 — Reaction:** Participant satisfaction (target: 4.5/5)
- **Level 2 — Learning:** Knowledge/skill acquisition (target: 85%+ assessment scores)
- **Level 3 — Behavior:** On-the-job application (target: 70%+ behavior change at 90 days)
- **Level 4 — Results:** Business impact (target: measurable ROI within 6 months)
